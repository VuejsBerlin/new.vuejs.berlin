Let's talk about Vue.js, its ecosystem and your own projects!

## Hack'n'Tell 💻

In this format, people share and discuss their projects in a loose, informal round; exchanging ideas, asking questions, and maybe even writing some code together. It's meant as a counterbalance to the otherwise (usually) more formal talks. Not everyone has the time, the slides, or the nerve to prepare a full presentation. This round is for you!

This will not replace the usual meetups. It is an experiment and if there is interest, we can decide, how to handle it in the future; either by making every third (or so) meetup a Hack'n'Tell or by introducing an additional event. In the meantime, please use [the chat](https://chat.vuejs.berlin/) to organize, discuss and hype yourself.
